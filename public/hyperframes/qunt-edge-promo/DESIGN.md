# Design System

## Overview

Qunt Edge uses a dark, product-led interface built around a void-black canvas, thin cobalt borders, compact dashboard surfaces, and dense trading analytics. The layout language is operational rather than editorial: terminal-like labels, clear data cards, miniature charts, and direct CTA surfaces. The brand should feel premium, restrained, and technical, with color used as signal rather than decoration.

## Colors

- **Void Canvas**: `#000000` - Primary background.
- **Obsidian Surface**: `#070914` - Main panel and card surface.
- **Raised Surface**: `#0B1020` - Elevated dashboard frames.
- **Cobalt Accent**: `#3B6CFF` - Primary action, active state, and electric highlights.
- **Cobalt Tint**: `#101A3A` - Subtle section and card fill.
- **Text Strong**: `#F7FAFF` - Main headings and critical values.
- **Text Muted**: `#9AA8C7` - Body copy and secondary labels.
- **Positive Signal**: `#42D98B` - Plan adherence and clean execution.
- **Warning Signal**: `#F4BE4A` - Risk drift and review alerts.
- **Negative Signal**: `#F06A6A` - Rule breaks and loss markers.

## Typography

- **Primary Sans**: Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui. Used for all titles, body, interface labels, and data values.
- **Heading Scale**: 88-104px for hero frames, 56-72px for beat statements, 30-38px for cards.
- **Interface Scale**: 16-20px labels, 22-28px card body, 34-58px key metrics.
- **Label Style**: All-caps, 0.12em to 0.18em letter spacing, medium or bold weight.

## Elevation

Depth is built with thin cobalt borders, inset highlights, and layered translucent cobalt surfaces. Shadows stay dark and quiet, with localized cobalt glow only behind active product states. No backdrop blur, no large fuzzy blur entrances, and no generic glassmorphism.

## Components

- **Hyperframe Browser Shell**: Rounded product frame with traffic-light controls, thin cobalt border, and a centered status pill.
- **Execution Audit Cards**: Compact metric cards showing PnL, plan adherence, risk drift, and review SLA.
- **Signal Rail**: Horizontal activity stream with clean, warning, and negative status dots.
- **Mini Chart Surface**: Bar and line chart hybrids that imply dashboard analytics without relying on screenshots.
- **AI Debrief Panel**: Dark command-style panel with concise recommendations and structured tags.
- **CTA Lockup**: Large product name, compact promise, and one cobalt action button.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use void black as the default canvas and cobalt as the main interactive signal.
- Keep panels compact, aligned, and dashboard-like.
- Use positive, warning, and negative colors only for trading state.
- Let motion feel like product telemetry: scanning, counting, sliding, and settling.

### Don'ts

- Do not use bright full-screen gradients or decorative blob backgrounds.
- Do not use backdrop blur or filter blur.
- Do not use stock imagery, generic finance photos, or abstract 3D objects.
- Do not make the promo feel like a landing-page slideshow.

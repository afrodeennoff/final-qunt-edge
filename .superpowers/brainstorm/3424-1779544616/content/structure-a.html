Chosen Direction: A — Ultra-Modern SaaS Homepage

Confirmed: We are going with Option A (Ultra-Modern SaaS) — clean, premium, breathing room, bold typography, subtle interactions. Fresh from-scratch redesign.

Proposed New Homepage Structure (High-Level)

Minimal, high-impact sections. Goal: fast scanning, strong emotional hook, clear value, and easy conversion.

  1. Hero (Above the fold)
    — Short powerful headline + subheadline
    — 2 clear CTAs (primary: "Open Free Account" / secondary: "Watch 60s Demo")
    — Subtle animated background or clean gradient + faint trading chart lines
  2. Trust Bar
    — "Trusted by 8,200+ funded traders" + logos of top prop firms + real-time metrics (e.g. "47,291 trades synced today")
  3. The Problem (Pain)
    — 3–4 crisp pain points in a clean grid (no heavy bento overload)
    — Short, relatable, professional tone
  4. The Solution (What Qunt Edge Actually Is)
    — One clear sentence: "The modern operating system for serious futures traders."
    — 4 minimal feature cards with icons + one-sentence benefit (not feature lists)
  5. Live in Action
    — Clean interactive dashboard preview (or video embed with play button)
    — "See exactly what your trading day looks like"
  6. How It Works (3 Simple Steps)
    — Connect broker → Import & analyze → Get insights & edge
    — Very light, not overwhelming
  7. Final CTA + Social Proof
    — Big "Start free — No card required" + small "Join 8,200+ traders who already upgraded their edge"

Design Language Goals

  • Very generous spacing (8pt grid)
  • High contrast typography (large headings, excellent readability)
  • Subtle cobalt accents only where they matter
  • Almost no clutter — every pixel earns its place
  • Mobile-first, buttery smooth

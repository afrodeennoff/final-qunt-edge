New Homepage Design Directions (From Scratch)

Based on your request for a complete new design from scratch + cleaning unused code.

Here are 3 high-level directions. Click one to select your preference (or describe your own).

A. Ultra-Modern SaaS (Recommended)

Style: Clean, premium, lots of breathing room, bold typography, subtle micro-interactions.

Hero: Powerful one-liner + clear value props + prominent "Get Started" / "See Live Demo"

Sections: Problem → Solution (with interactive preview) → Trust (real metrics + logos) → Features (minimal 4 cards) → How it works (simple 3-step) → Final CTA

Pros: Feels fresh, professional, easy to maintain, high conversion.

Cons: Less "trader terminal" vibe.

B. Dark Trading Command Center

Style: Deep black + electric accents, dense but organized, terminal-inspired elements.

Hero: "Your edge starts here" + live trading stats strip + "Open Dashboard" primary CTA

Sections: Pain points (raw & honest) → Core features with mini demos → Real trader stories → Advanced tools → Pricing tease

Pros: Matches current "pro trader" identity, immersive.

Cons: Can feel heavy, harder to scan, more maintenance.

C. Trust & Content Heavy

Style: Balanced, educational marketing site with strong social proof.

Hero: Big headline + stats + "Join 8,000+ funded traders"

Sections: Why traders choose us → Prop firm integrations → Results & case studies → Educational resources → Community → Final CTA

Pros: Great for SEO and long-term trust.

Cons: Can feel like "another marketing site", slower to load if not careful.

My recommendation: Start with A (Ultra-Modern SaaS) — it gives the biggest "from scratch" refresh while still feeling powerful for professional traders. We can always add trading terminal touches where it makes sense.
